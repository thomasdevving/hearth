# Hearth for clinicians: script (2:50)

`video/clinicians/hearth-for-clinicians.mp4` (1920×1080) explains Hearth to GPs, therapists,
psychiatrists and researchers: how the relapse-prevention plan is made, what the app does with
it, why it should work, where its clinical boundaries are and what they get. It ends with two
questions they can answer by email, and the offer of a 15-minute call.

- Every point is written on screen, so the video works without sound (most people watch a
  link from an email muted).
- **VO** is an optional voice-over. At about 2.5 words per second every line fits its slot.
- All phone screens are the iOS app in the iOS 27 Simulator, on the simulated person Sam
  (`assets/ios-*.jpg`). The plan cards come from a debug stand-in for the on-device model
  (`-HearthDemoCards`), which adds one sign Sam never wrote so the quote check shows.
- Sources are the ones on the app's Science page (`apps/web/src/screens/Science.tsx`).
- Calm background music is generated by `music.py` (no samples, no licence questions) and mixed
  in at about -20 LUFS. To use another track, replace `music.wav` in `build.cjs`.

Regenerate:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8090 --bind 127.0.0.1
```
```bash
node video/clinicians/build.cjs
```
